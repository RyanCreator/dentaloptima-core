Dental activity processing comments

This guide contains a list of comments received on your `SCHEDL' or `DAILYS' file, or on a dentists Schedule.
Comments are provided to give additional details around your data submission, these are for information only.
If the information does not provide the details you're looking for you can contact us.
Our Customer Contact Centre is open 8am to 6pm Monday to Friday.
