Dental activity processing errors

This guide contains a list of dental activity processing errors, and provides details on how to correct them.
In this guide, there is listed general and orthodontic errors you might experience in England, Wales or the Isle of Man.
If you are struggling to correct an activity processing error after following the steps outlined in this guide, you can contact us.
Our Customer Contact Centre is open 8am to 6pm Monday to Friday.
